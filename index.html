You are not invisible. You are not the problem.

The job search is not hard because you did not try hard enough. It is hard because the system makes you do the same work over and over, then leaves you waiting.

The silence

You send application after application and hear nothing back.

Not a no. Just nothing. So you keep applying, and you start to wonder if anyone saw it at all.

The scatter

Five tabs. Four portals. Spreadsheets, screenshots, and saved passwords.

No one place tells you where anything stands, or what to do next.

The void

You tailor every resume, then it disappears into a system that never tells you why.

Same effort, same silence, no way to learn from it.

One profile. Employers find you. You decide.

GradHarbor flips the job search around. Instead of you chasing every opening, your profile is the application.

Step 1

One profile

Build one profile once. Your skills, your experience, and the kind of work you are looking for — in one place.

Step 2

Employers find you

Employers see people who fit the role, starting with skills — not a name, a school, or a graduation year.

Step 3

You approve every application

An employer can invite you. Nothing is sent without your yes. No applications you did not choose.

For students and recent grads

Stop re-entering the same information on every site. Keep one profile, see jobs that fit your skills, and choose what to apply to.

When an employer reaches out, you will know why they found you — the skills that matched, in plain words.

For employers

See candidates you would otherwise never find, starting with what they can do.

Invite the people who fit. Every application you receive was approved by the candidate first.

Built by someone applying right now.

GradHarbor is being built by a BYU senior who is in the job search himself — building the thing he wishes existed, for the people going through it with him.

No tricks. No shortcuts. Just a fairer way to be seen.
